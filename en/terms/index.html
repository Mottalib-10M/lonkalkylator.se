Vanliga frågor

Are the Swedish net pay results binding?
No. Every figure is an estimate for information and does not replace advice from Skatteverket or an authorised tax adviser. Small gaps with a real payslip usually come from rounding or from tax tables assuming an even income over the year; larger ones nearly always come from a benefit, an adjustment or a deduction.
May I embed the calculator on my own site?
Yes. The widget is free to embed, including on commercial sites and with no limit on traffic, on one condition: the attribution link in its footer must stay visible and clickable. That link replaces any fee or registration, and the widget loads the current tax rates itself, so it never goes out of date on your page.
